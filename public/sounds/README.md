`keyboard-click-sprite.wav` contains short cuts from the IBM M2 typing recording.

Source: [IBM M2 sound on Wikimedia Commons](https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:IBM_M2_sound.ogg)
The recording is released under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).

`cd-metal-click.mp3` is the same CC0 metal-click sample used by the CD-style hero artwork at [chanhdai.com](https://chanhdai.com/). The sample is credited to Kenney in the [source component](https://github.com/ncdai/chanhdai.com/blob/main/src/lib/soundcn/metal-click.ts).
